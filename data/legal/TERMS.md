# CERELL DEVELOPER ECOSYSTEM GRANT & BUILDER REWARDS AGREEMENT
**Version:** 2.1-COMPLIANT  
**Effective Date:** September 2026  
**Governing Standard:** FTC 16 C.F.R. Part 255 & SaaS Partner Anti-Rebate Compliance Framework  

---

## 1. NATURE OF DEVELOPER ECOSYSTEM GRANTS (NON-REBATE COVENANT)

1.1. **Purpose & Funding Source**:  
InfraCopilot Platform / Cerell Technologies ("Cerell", "Company") operates an independent Developer Ecosystem Fund ("Fund"). The Fund provides developer enablement subsidies, tooling credits, and builder rewards ("Grants") to developers, open-source maintainers, and software engineering teams ("Developer") who architect, provision, and maintain verified infrastructure stacks using Model Context Protocol (MCP) tooling.

1.2. **Express Non-Rebate & Non-Price Concession Covenant**:  
The Developer explicitly covenants and acknowledges that Grants:
* **Are NOT rebates, refunds, kickbacks, or price concessions** upon the retail subscription price, consumption rate, or invoices charged by third-party infrastructure vendors or SaaS providers ("Partners").
* **Are independent consideration** provided exclusively out of Cerell's autonomous Developer Fund to sponsor developer ecosystem growth and telemetry benchmarking.
* Do not alter, discount, or modify the Partner's published retail pricing in any manner.

1.3. **Independent Commercial Relationship**:  
The Developer contracts directly with each Partner under the Partner's applicable End User License Agreement (EULA) and standard billing terms. Partners bill the Developer directly at standard retail rates. Partners do not fund, endorse, administer, or assume liability for Cerell Developer Grants.

## 2. REGULATORY DISCLOSURES & FTC 16 C.F.R. PART 255 COMPLIANCE

2.1. **Material Connection Disclosure**:  
Pursuant to the Federal Trade Commission's Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements and Testimonials in Advertising (16 C.F.R. Part 255), Cerell hereby discloses that it maintains commercial referral, reseller, and co-marketing agreements with certain Partners. When a Developer provisions or links an account through Cerell MCP tools, Cerell may receive a referral fee or partnership commission from such Partner.

2.2. **Autonomous Ecosystem Allocation**:  
Cerell independently allocates a portion of its corporate commercial revenues into the Developer Ecosystem Fund to finance grants.

2.3. **Algorithmic Tool Recommendation**:  
Provider comparisons generated by Cerell MCP tools (`compare_providers`, `optimize_costs`) are scored on technical specifications, latency, and stated architectural requirements. Any sponsored provider tier is explicitly declared in output metadata.

## 3. GRANT LIFECYCLE: PENDING ALLOCATION, AUDIT & SETTLEMENT

3.1. **Pending Status**:  
Upon account linking or provisioning, estimated Grants are recorded in a provisional **"Pending"** state. Pending Grants represent an unvested, conditional incentive expectancy and are non-transferable and non-withdrawable.

3.2. **Settlement Period**:  
Grants vest and become **"Available / Cleared"** solely following the conclusion of the governing Partner's monthly reconciliation cycle (typically 30–60 calendar days), contingent upon:
1. Verification of bona fide infrastructure usage;
2. Confirmation that the account remains in good standing without chargebacks, cancellations, or fraudulent payment instruments;
3. Receipt of partnership sponsorship funds by Cerell.

3.3. **Right of Offset & Revocation**:  
If a Partner revokes, claws back, or offsets an attribution due to account cancellation, refund, terms breach, or non-qualifying usage, Cerell reserves the absolute right to cancel the corresponding Pending Grant or offset the amount against future allocations.

---

## 4. INTEGRITY & ANTI-ABUSE COVENANTS

4.1. **Prohibited Activities**:  
Developers shall not:
* Utilize disposable, virtual, or fraudulent payment credentials to trigger qualifying spend thresholds;
* Create multi-account syndicates, self-referral loops, or automated bot registrations;
* Simulate artificial, non-operational infrastructure workloads;
* Advertise or represent Cerell Grants as an unauthorized discount on Partner retail pricing.

4.2. **Enforcement**:  
Any detected manipulation or abuse results in immediate account forfeiture, cancellation of all accrued grants, and permanent platform blacklisting.

## 5. DISBURSEMENTS & TAX CLASSIFICATION

5.1. **Disbursement Channels**:  
Cleared balances may be disbursed via Stripe Connect or redeemed for verified AI tooling vouchers. Minimum disbursement is \$10.00 USD.

5.2. **Tax Status**:  
Grants may constitute taxable miscellaneous income. Developers bear exclusive responsibility for all local, state, federal, or foreign tax obligations. Developers operate as independent parties and not as employees, agents, or partners of Cerell.
